It's my first post!

I wanted the first post to be special. Let me fill you in with what I've been up to, in no order.

A friendly ACEZ (dearALICE fan) proxy bought me three copies of dearALICE’s signed Maxi Single. It was unfortunately only available to UK only customers. I bought sleeves for it and used the soldering iron to resize it. It looks sleek with sleeves. It makes me want to sleeve everything.

I was trying to find a solution for having a majority FLAC music library and wanting to use iTunes to sync my iPod. I didn’t want to keep two copies of my library and 3rd party syncing software is lacking or out of date.

I found this program called ffmpegfs on github through my searches on Reddit. ffmpegfs is basically a virtual filesystem that automatically transcode video or audio files to many formats as it’s being accessed. I would use it as any audio file being live transcoded to ALAC, with iTunes not knowing any difference.

It is a Linux program first but I’m a Windows user. The developer had Windows directions on how to build it, and man...... That was a lot of effort and turns out the Windows branch isn’t full progressed that it’s super slow. [Reminder to self, go through Windows build directions and update the documentation and submit a commit]

I ended up figuring out how to use it on Linux but turns out the files were output in a way that iTunes didn’t like. After a lengthy discussion with the developer, they ended up adding a new feature that would make it compatible with iTunes. Other than having to build it from source, since there’s not a new release for it, it is wonderful!!

I haven’t shared this with the developer yet, but I am able to build and run ffmpegfs in Debian with Windows WSL. I tried the Ubuntu WSL but it had some weird escape character issue when building the program. Maybe once a release that has the ALAC feature, I’m using is out, I can use Ubuntu WSL to run it.

 

ffmpegfs on GitHub

I’ve been wanting to get a new camera. I currently have a Sony NEX 5-TL with a basic little zoom lens Sony SLEP1650. The camera body is a little busted and I’m having to use painters’ tape to keep the battery compartment closed. I’ve also been wanting more zoom on the lens, because that just seems cool.

I asked around on reddit and concluded that: Cameras and lens hold their value. Another reason I’ve been interested in cameras again is that the selfie camera on my Note 20 Ultra 5g is super lacking, and taking a selfie with the back camera is a skill I’ve yet to master.

I ultimately found out about the Sony RX100 cameras, it’s basically a Sony Point and Shoot camera with 7 different models. The last model, VII was launched in 2019 and no new version since. Like I mentioned above, cameras hold their value, this camera is $1200 USD and I’ve been seeing used go for $900, though this is for the last latest model.

I am thinking of selling my current camera and lens to get this. I played with a demo unit of it at a Best Buy and it felt nice. The size is great.

I’ve gone back to carrying a sling bag, man-purse, purse. I saw these two travel bags on clearance at Target and thought about it for like a week. I bought it a different Target than where I saw it first, and that one had a defect, so I exchanged it with from the first target. I also bought the squarer bag just in case I need to carry more things.

Having a purse has been a life changer, I can keep my wallet in it, and other items like chapstick, earplugs, a camcorder (lol) and a safety knife. This saved me at the movie theater when I watched the IVE THE 1ST WORLD TOUR in CINEMA film. The crowd screams through the movie theater speakers made my ears ring. I was watching thinking, I wish I had my concert ear plugs, then realized 2nd song in, OH THEY ARE IN MY PURSE THAT IS WITH ME! Saved me on that.

Hope you enjoy your stay on my website and come again.
